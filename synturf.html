Synturf Digital Transformation


The Challenge

Synturf, a premier Australian provider of synthetic grass, faced the challenge of standing out in a highly competitive landscaping and sports surfacing market. Their existing digital presence needed to bridge the gap between two distinct audiences: residential homeowners seeking DIY or low-maintenance luxury, and commercial/sports entities requiring high-performance, technical specifications. The primary goal was to transform a product-heavy catalog into a conversion-driven digital platform that simplified the selection process for "Australian-made" quality.


The Strategy

The strategy centered on "Simplifying the Selection," ensuring that users could identify the right product for their specific needs—whether it be pet-friendly backyards, heat-reductive pool surrounds, or FIFA-grade sports fields.

  • Segmented User Journeys: Creating distinct pathways for "Landscaping" (Residential) vs. "Sports Surfaces" to reduce cognitive load and move users toward the appropriate quote funnel.
  • Value-First Messaging: Elevating key selling points—100% Australian-made, 10-year warranties, and UV protection—to build immediate trust and justify a premium price point.
  • Educational Content Strategy: Implementing a "Product Spotlight" approach for flagship offerings like Everest (Cooling Technology) and Fusion (Natural Aesthetic) to move beyond generic "artificial grass" descriptions.

The Execution

I designed a modern, responsive website that balanced tactical utility with high-end lifestyle aspiration:

  • Visual Catalog Design: Developed a clean, grid-based layout for product pages (e.g., Summit, Ezyturf) featuring high-resolution close-ups and clear technical "Specifications" tables to assist both DIYers and contractors.
  • Direct Conversion Funnel: laced "Request a Quote" as a persistent, high-visibility CTA, supported by a streamlined lead-capture form to reduce drop-off rates.
  • Component-Based Layouts: Built the site using scalable components, allowing for "Recent Projects" galleries to be easily updated, showcasing real-world applications from tennis courts to backyard renovations.
  • Mobile-First Optimisation: Optimised the interface for on-site use, recognising that many customers and installers would be accessing product data and measurement guides while in their gardens or at job sites.


The Results

  • Enhanced Product Clarity: Successfully differentiated four core products (Everest, Fusion, Summit, Ezyturf) through unique value propositions, leading to more qualified lead inquiries.
  • Strengthened Brand Authority: Positioned Synturf as a technical expert in "Cooling Technology" and durable Australian manufacturing, distancing them from cheaper, low-quality imports.
  • Improved User Engagement: By streamlining the navigation into "Sports" and "Leisure," the site successfully serves a dual-market audience without one diluting the other.
  • Seamless Lead Generation: Created an end-to-end digital touchpoint that guides users from initial curiosity (Benefits) to technical validation (Specifications) and final action (Quote Request).
